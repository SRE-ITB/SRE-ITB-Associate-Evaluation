## Goal
Three templates that scripts can fill automatically without breaking the layout.

## Context
Scripts replace text placeholders and one image. Anything the script does not replace must look fine as is. Follow the SRE ITB visual identity guideline (Design division GDV).

## Templates

| Template | Format | Placeholders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Birthday poster | Google Slides, 1 slide, 1080x1350 (Instagram portrait) | `{{name}}`, `{{division}}`, image `{{photo}}` |
| Best Staff poster | Google Slides, 1 slide, 1080x1350 | `{{name}}`, `{{division}}`, `{{period}}`, image `{{photo}}` |
| Report card | Google Docs, A4 | `{{name}}`, `{{nim}}`, `{{division}}`, `{{period}}`, `{{i1}}`..`{{i4}}`, `{{total}}`, `{{average}}`, `{{status}}`, `{{comments}}` |

## Rules for templates

- Text placeholders are typed literally, for example `{{name}}`, inside a text box sized for long names (test with 35 characters).
- The photo is a normal image (any placeholder picture) whose alt text title is `{{photo}}`. Use a fixed square crop frame.
- Report card: `{{comments}}` sits in its own paragraph and may expand to several lines. `{{status}}` may be empty, so the layout must not leave an awkward gap.
- Avoid custom fonts that the org account cannot use. Stick to fonts available in Google Slides/Docs.
