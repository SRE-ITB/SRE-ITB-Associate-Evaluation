## Goal
Every system file lives in the SRE ITB Shared drive, managed by the SRE ITB Google account, with access set for the team.

## Context
Triggers run as the account that installs them. Files in a Shared drive belong to the drive, files in My Drive belong to their creator. If anything is owned by a personal account, it breaks when that person leaves. See D-015 and D-031.

## Suggested steps
1. Check the org account recovery options (phone, recovery email). They must not be tied to one person who leaves this term. Record who holds access.
2. From the org account, create the Drive layout in `docs/01-architecture.md` section 1 inside `SRE ITB 2026/2027 / MEDCOMM / IT DEVELOPMENT`. Check who can open `IT DEVELOPMENT`; everyone there inherits access to `Master`.
3. Share `System/` and `Templates/` with Dev A, Dev B, Dev C, designer as Editor. Share `DEV/` with devs.
4. Write down the org account handover owner and process in a private note for the lead (not in this repo).

## Boundary
Do not store passwords or recovery codes in the repo or in group chats.
