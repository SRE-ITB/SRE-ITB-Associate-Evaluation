## Goal
Every system file is owned by the SRE ITB Google account, with access set for the team.

## Context
Triggers run as the account that installs them, and files belong to their creator. If anything is owned by a personal account, it breaks when that person leaves. See D-015.

## Suggested steps
1. Check the org account recovery options (phone, recovery email). They must not be tied to one person who leaves this term. Record who holds access.
2. From the org account, create the Drive layout in `docs/01-architecture.md` section 1.
3. Share `System/` and `Templates/` with Dev A, Dev B, designer as Editor. Share `DEV/` with devs.
4. Write down the org account handover owner and process in a private note for the lead (not in this repo).

## Boundary
Do not store passwords or recovery codes in the repo or in group chats.

## Done =
- [ ] Drive layout exists, owned by the org account.
- [ ] Team has the access listed above.
- [ ] Recovery options checked and the handover owner is recorded privately.
