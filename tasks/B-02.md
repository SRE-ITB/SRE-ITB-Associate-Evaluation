## Goal
Every morning, each active staff with a birthday today gets a poster in Drive, and OM receives one email listing them.

## Context
- Rules: `docs/00-scope.md` section 11.
- Template placeholders (agree with the designer): text `{{name}}`, `{{division}}`, and one image shape whose alt text title is `{{photo}}`.
- Used in production from 1 December, so this is on the critical path.

## Suggested steps
1. `Posters.gs`: `renderPoster(templateId, replacements, photoFileId, outFolderId, fileName)` copies the Slides template, replaces text, replaces the `{{photo}}` image, exports page 1 as PNG, saves it, trashes the temp copy. Reused by B-03.
2. PNG export options: the Slides advanced service `Presentations.Pages.getThumbnail`, or the export URL with an OAuth token via `UrlFetchApp`. Pick one, note the choice in the PR.
3. `dailyBirthday()`: find active staff whose birth month and day equal today in Asia/Jakarta. Feb 29 birthdays match Feb 28 in non-leap years.
4. Skip staff already in `BirthdayLog` for today.
5. Render posters into `Birthday/<YYYY-MM>/`, append to `BirthdayLog`.
6. Send one email to `OM_EMAILS` with names, divisions, and Drive links. No email if nobody has a birthday.
7. If one poster fails (for example an unreadable photo), still process the rest, and list the failure in the email.

## Boundary
Do not email staff directly. Do not post to any chat or social media.
